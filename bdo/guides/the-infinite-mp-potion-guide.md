#### Infinite MP Potion NPC locations

Below is the location of **NPC Merindora**, you can speak to her to convert ancient pieces like mentioned above. You must also show her all 3 potion pieces before you are able to exchange items for the **Night Crow's Dawn Stone&#x20;**&#x61;nd the Krogdalo's Protection Stone.

Speak to NPC **Merindora** and complete the quest **Odore's Spirit Essence** while here, this will later allow you to hand in your [Rumbling Earth Shards]()

![](media:c68c790a-4b56-4824-ad15-b01f025c4b58)

After showing NPC Merindora all three potion pieces, you can visit **Ominous** **__Altar__ (Garmoth's Nest node manager)** at the location below and exchange Dragon Scale Fossil x100 to obtain Night Crow's Dawn Stone

![](media:562bcf4f-f3ad-472b-b00f-8dd8d1b577ac)

Finally is **__Krogdalo's Trace__ (Krogdalo's Trace node manager)**, who can exchange **Rumbling Earth Shard x100 for** Krogdalo's Protection Ston&#x65

![](media:a6c5cbad-6b3b-4253-a01e-dc63b1090a9e)


## Atanis' Element

Atanis Element is an exchangable item that can be exchanged for pity pieces of any infinite potion at a 1 to 1 ratio.
