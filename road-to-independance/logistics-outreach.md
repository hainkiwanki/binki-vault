# Logistics conversations: script

Goal: find a painful, repeated problem in small warehouses that someone would pay to fix. Not to sell anything yet.

## Who to contact
Owners and managers of small warehouses, 3PLs and food wholesalers, roughly 5-50 staff, within reach of me. Skip anyone linked to RealityMatters or its clients.

## Message 1: LinkedIn (English)
> Hi [first name], I grew up around a frozen-food warehouse and I'm now a developer. I'm researching what eats the most time in small warehouses day to day. Would you have 15 minutes for a call or a coffee? I'm not selling anything, I just want to learn how it really works. Thank you!

## Message 2: email (English)
Subject: A few questions about running a small warehouse
> Hello [first name], I grew up around a frozen-food warehouse and I'm now a developer. I'm researching what takes the most time in small warehouses day to day, and I'd value your view. > 2. What was the most annoying mistake or delay last month?
> 3. What do you wish a computer did for you?

No walk-ins and no cold calls. Written replies are fine: email answers are useful data, and the full question list can follow if someone agrees to talk.

## Questions for a call or a longer written exchange (ask in this order, listen, take notes)
1. Walk me through a normal day. Where does the time go?
2. How do orders reach you (phone, email, PDF, a system)? What happens next?
3. What gets typed in by hand more than once?
4. What went wrong last month, and what did it cost (time, money, a customer)?
5. How do you check what arrives against what was ordered?
6. How do you track stock, expiry dates and what to ship first?
7. What do customers keep calling to ask?
8. What tools do you use today, and what do you dislike about them?
9. Have you tried to fix any of this? What happened?
10. If one thing disappeared from your week, what would it be?
11. Who else should I talk to?

## What to record after each conversation
- Date, name, role, company size, type of goods
- The top 3 problems in their words
- Hours per week they lose to each (their estimate)
- Tools used today
- Whether they said they'd pay, and how much (only if they offered it)
- Who they suggested next

## Rules
- Don't pitch. If they ask what you're building, say you're still exploring.
- Don't mention or use anything from RealityMatters or its clients.
- Thank them and send a short note afterwards.
- A problem counts as real if 3 different people name it unprompted.

## Outreach tracker
Email and LinkedIn only. Target: about 30-40 messages for a handful of replies. One follow-up after about a week, once.
