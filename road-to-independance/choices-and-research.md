## 3. Services and offers

### Pricing logic
- Price follows how costly the buyer's problem is: businesses pay hundreds to thousands, hobbyists EUR 3-7.
- Arithmetic (assumptions, unverified): about 12 billable h/week x EUR 50-80/h = EUR 2,400-3,800/month.

### Website model (user, 17 Nov)
- Simple sites about EUR 500; database-backed sites cost more; integrations cost a lot more.
- Small client change requests automated into pull requests (Claude Code on a ticket or email, owner reviews and merges).
- Clients may host themselves: accepted, at a cost to profit.
- Languages: Dutch, French, English.
- Low-ticket and crowded market (Wix, Squarespace, agencies, AI builders): fine as an entry offer, weak as the main business. Door-opener idea: website plus one automation (booking, quotes, forms, invoicing) with a care fee.
- Unverified prices: websites EUR 500-2,000 one-off, care plan EUR 20-100/month.
- Open: EUR 500 only pays if a simple site takes about 8 hours or less including content chasing and revisions. The recurring care plan is the real income. A git-based CMS (client edits text alone) may remove most change requests.

### Logistics wedge hypotheses (to check in conversations, not facts)
- Orders arriving by email or PDF and re-typed by hand, receiving documents, expiry and FIFO handling, pallet tracking.
- Contacts come from the family company (frozen fast-food-ingredients warehouse, no longer in the family) and public sources.
- A problem is real when 3 different people name it unprompted.

## 5. Immigration ledger (Korea, F-1-D Workation / digital nomad)
Source: https://bel.mofa.go.kr/be-en/brd/m_28171/view.do?seq=18&page=1 (Embassy of Korea in Belgium; written/modified 2026-09-30; Korean version official). Summarised by fetch agent: verify on the page.
- **Embassy reply (16 Nov):** F-1-D open to employees of foreign companies and self-employed individuals and business owners; pointed to the web page. Did not answer income, history, whose-name or structure.
- **Eligibility on page:** over 18; overseas business owner or employee of foreign company (Korean-company employees ineligible); can work remotely; more than 1 year in the same industry. "Freelancers are not eligible" (conflicts with email wording).
- **Minimum annual income (before tax):** age 18-34: KRW 78,624,000 (Seoul/Incheon/Gyeonggi) or 52,416,000 (reduced-threshold regions); age 35+: KRW 104,832,000 or 78,624,000. With dependants the 35+ threshold applies. Rough EUR 30-70k/yr: convert at the current rate.
- **Evidence:** 12 months pay-slips (nothing stated for business owners); employment certificate (1 year same industry, remote ability); 3 months bank statements in own name; Belgian criminal record with apostille (under 6 months); English insurance minimum EUR 70,000 for 1 year; passport, photo, form 17.
- **Stay:** 1 year + extension up to 1 year = 2 years total (not the 3-year headline). Register within 90 days if staying over 91 days. Employment inside Korea strictly restricted; Korean clients not stated.
- **Not stated:** processing time, application method, translation, business-owner income evidence.
- **Follow-up sent 16 Nov:** is a Belgian sole proprietorship ("zelfstandige in bijberoep") selling digital products worldwide a business owner or a freelancer? Is a BV required? Reply: ____ ; follow up 23 Nov.
- **Still to ask after reply:** income evidence without pay-slips; personal income vs company profit; savings substituting; proof of 1 year same industry; whether current salary counts.
- **Planning impact (17 Nov):** user is 31 (born 1994), so the 18-34 bracket applies (35+ only with dependants, or from 2029). Threshold: KRW 52.4M/yr (about EUR 33k, 2,700/month) in reduced regions, KRW 78.6M/yr (about EUR 49k, 4,100/month) in Seoul/Incheon/Gyeonggi, assuming about 1,600 KRW/EUR (verify rate). A EUR 5,000/month target clears both. The real obstacle is documenting income: the page asks for 12 months of pay-slips and says nothing for business owners, and current take-home is about EUR 2,000/month. Structure may need to produce documentable income (possibly a company paying salary). No company until the embassy answers (lightest compliant structure rule). Decide target region in the worksheet.

## 6. Belgium registration and tax (research from blogs, NOT official)
- Ongoing sales are business activity, so CBE registration regardless of amount.
- EUR 1,922.16/yr (2026) is the social-contribution floor for side activity, not a registration threshold.
- VAT threshold: sources conflict (EUR 25,000 exemption vs register regardless). Verify.
- Plan: register as "zelfstandige in bijberoep" online via a registration service (helps with these questions, per user; cost and scope unverified). Ask them: fees, first contributions before any income, when invoicing is allowed, what advice they give, switching to BV later, and whether selling before registration is complete is allowed.
- **Registration timing (open):** user intended to register after the first sale. Plan rule says registration and invoicing must be ready before taking money; Belgian rules likely require registration before ongoing sales; platforms report payouts (DAC7). Treat payment as blocked until registered; non-binding buyer commitments are allowed meanwhile. For dev services, registration is needed before invoicing (unverified; confirm with the registration service).
